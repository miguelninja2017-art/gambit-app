GAMBIT privacy policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

GAMBIT is a private, single-user assistant. Its only user is its owner.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, GAMBIT reads and manages the owner's own Gmail, Google Calendar and contacts so it can show, draft and send messages and manage events. Nothing is sent, changed or deleted without the owner's approval.

What happens to the data

Data is used only to carry out the owner's requests. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. Sign-in keys are stored encrypted on the owner's own devices and server. Requests are processed by the AI service the owner uses (Anthropic's Claude API) solely to answer the owner.

Google user data

GAMBIT's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke GAMBIT's access at any time from the Google Account permissions page.

Contact

Use the support email shown on GAMBIT's Google sign-in screen.
